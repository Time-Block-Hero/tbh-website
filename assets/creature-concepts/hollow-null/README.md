# Hollow-Null concept sheets

Generated with the built-in ImageGen workflow using the retained Industrial Sci-Fi Anime template reference.

## Canonical visual rules

- Hollow-Null bodies assemble around a literal causal void that reflects no light. The void is missing reality, not black skin, smoke, a portal, or a starfield.
- Bone-white ceramic, corroded machinery, translucent technical membranes, and displaced fragments may coexist without forming a coherent natural anatomy.
- Lost, Flying, and Roaring forms are parallel expressions of a Time-Death disaster. They are not evolutionary stages.
- A Hollow Time Lord is the singular causal core and stabilizing attractor of one disaster, not an evolved creature or biological ruler.

## Files and generation briefs

- `lost-hollow-null-concept-sheet.png` — Dominant asymmetric ground form with a compact torso void; incompatible ceramic, pale biological, mechanical, and membrane fragments; inset studies of void assembly and frame-skipped motion.
- `flying-hollow-null-concept-sheet.png` — Distinct crescent-like aerial form with no ordinary wings; fragments occupy mutually exclusive trajectories around the void; inset flight-silhouette and discontinuous-position studies.
- `roaring-hollow-null-concept-sheet.png` — Broad braced disorder-wave form with no mouth; its causal front separates fasteners, corrodes structures, and breaks alignment before sound arrives; inset core and environmental-effect studies.
- `hollow-time-lord-concept-sheet.png` — Colossal pseudo-regal disaster core with five crownlike spires, cathedral machinery, incomplete arms, orbital structures, and a city-scale torso void; inset crown/core and scale studies.

All sheets use a landscape 3:2 artbook layout, cold charcoal and dirty-white foundations, restrained rust and cyan-white rim light, tactile industrial materials, and no readable text, arrows, UI, card frame, logo, or watermark.
