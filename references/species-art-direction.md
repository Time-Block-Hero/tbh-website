# Original Species Art Direction

This derived guide follows `references/setting-art-contract.md`, `bestiary-taxonomy.js` and the source dossiers in `bestiary-data.js`. Race-wide principles, branch-specific standards and individual examples have different scopes. For Hollow-Nulls it defines recognition and derivation rules, not a stable species anatomy.

## 天使机体 / Angel Chassis

> Canonical design language approved. The eight references in `assets/creature-concepts/celestial-angel/explorations-round-2/` are compatible expressions of one ascension-vessel family, not competing species or fixed ranks.

### Canonical body plan

- Every Angel is a legless floating carrier for an ascended human consciousness. A serene, expressive biomimetic face and elegant humanoid upper body preserve age, gender, hair, and identity cues chosen by the uploaded person; the body below the waist becomes a purpose-built antigravity vessel rather than humanoid legs.
- The shared material balance is roughly seventy percent smooth living porcelain and mechanical ceramic to thirty percent restrained visible technology. Pearl white, opal, pale alloy, graphite joints, fine warm-gold seams, and cyan-to-violet star-energy light form the family palette.
- A compact jewel-like consciousness core is embedded in the sternum or central torso. It may be architecturally framed and visibly engineered, but never opens into a large engine bay, mechanical rib cage, or exposed chest cavity.
- A radiant halo, crown, mandorla, or orbital structure must remain readable around the head or body. It expresses both divinity and functional mind coherence, communication, cooling, computation, and attitude control.
- “Wings” are a configurable sacred system rather than one fixed anatomy. Approved forms include translucent photonic wings, mobile aegis shields, articulated solar sails, floating star-reliquaries or restrained remote cannons, energy-ribbon fins, and segmented orbital-cathedral structures. Their silhouette must remain numinous before it reads as military hardware.
- Approved lower vessels include concentric hover discs, gyroscopic astrolabes, comet cores, resonance bells, toroidal reliquaries, and open constellation thrones. Petal skirts are optional, never mandatory. Across all variants, the biomimetic face, ceramic torso, compact core, legless hover transition, and halo signal are the family invariants.

### Role derivation

- **天使—星光开悟者 / Starlight Enlightener:** emphasize a human-identifiable biomimetic face, translucent star-energy computation lens, solar-sail or energy-ribbon wings, and an astrolabe or comet-core vessel. Accumulated movement should visibly align constellations and concentrate star-energy into insight.
- **天使—阵线统御官 / Frontline Commander:** use mobile aegis wings, relay prisms, and an expanded crown or constellation throne to gather fallen battlefield nodes, preserve memories, and route a reserve body. This is technological continuity of consciousness, not necromancy.
- **上位天使—炮台巡游者 / Upper Angel—Turret Roamer:** use detached star-reliquaries or sleek remote emitters as a wing constellation around a serene Angel body. The devices may align and fire after sustained movement, but they must remain elegant sacred instruments subordinate to the ascension-vessel silhouette. “Upper” denotes payload grade, not evolution or greater personhood.

### Avoid

- Humanoid legs or feet, predatory lower limbs, a huge exposed chest mechanism, skeletal ribs, generic giant-robot bulk, assassin styling, sinister red eyes, medieval plate or church vestments, and weapons that overwhelm the face, halo, or floating sacred silhouette. Conventional feathers may inspire energy or ceramic geometry but must not turn the chassis into an ordinary fantasy angel.

## 天庭机灵 / Celestial Sprite

### Canonical body plan

- Every Sprite uses the same permanently hovering compact frame, about 45 centimeters across: a rounded pearl-white teardrop body, one expressive cyan visor, two long swept-back sensor ear-fins, an oval belly learning-core, two tiny three-finger tool hands, and a ventral antigravity aperture.
- Sensor fins are engineered communications and ranging arrays, not rabbit ears. Charm comes from round proportions, curious body tilts, timing, and visor response rather than fur, plush surfaces, infant anatomy, or limbs.
- Sprites never have legs, feet, wheels, walking poses, or ground contact. Accumulated movement deploys concentric hover rings and two vector fins in sequence: quiet drift, agile cruise, then high-speed route traversal. The chassis never grows or transforms into another creature.
- Future job variants may swap only the backpack hardpoint, tool hands, or compact cargo pod. The face shell, sensor fins, torso core, hip width, and leg proportions remain invariant.

Show [quiet drift / agile cruise / high-speed traversal] through progressively deployed concentric hover rings and vector fins, while the body proportions remain unchanged and the machine never touches the ground. Add only [role-specific backpack, tool, or cargo module]. Premium industrial sci-fi anime rendering in the Machine Celestial Court pearl-white, ice-blue, cobalt, graphite, cyan, and restrained warm-gold palette. Adorable through round proportions, intelligent posture, and visor expression; absolutely no legs, feet, wheels, fur, or walking poses. Artwork only; no readable text, logo, watermark, UI, card frame, decorative border, arrows, cost, or stat icons.

## 空亡体 / Hollow-Null

### Approved visual baselines

- `assets/card-art/lost-hollow-null/lost-hollow-null-01.png`: human-scale/common baseline.
- `assets/card-art/hollow-time-lord/hollow-time-lord-01.png`: colossal/Legendary baseline.

### Ontology

Hollow-Nulls are not organisms, invaders from a stable ecosystem, or a natural evolutionary lineage. They are causal debris created when mutually contradictory timeline states are compressed inside a rift. Each form must be derived from its particular contradiction and disaster environment. Lost, flying, and roaring forms are parallel expressions of a Time-Death event, not stages of growth. Greater power means stronger instability and wider causal failure—not thicker armor, older age, or a biologically superior body.

### Universal recognition rules

- Every form is organized by a perfectly lightless **causal void**. It must read as missing reality rather than black paint, smoke, a dark organ, or a portal with a visible interior.
- Anatomy and materials do not align: biological tissue, ceramic shell, machinery, architecture, or local debris may occupy mutually incompatible positions. No single material family, face, limb count, palette, or silhouette is universal.
- The same limb or fragment may appear in contradictory temporal positions. Movement skips frames and intermediate motion; nearby straight lines, reflections, lighting, memories, and action order may break.
- Surfaces visibly grow and erode at the same time. Physical damage matters less than whether the causal void continues to organize the surrounding fragments.
- The environment must reveal the specific disorder being produced. Do not add generic floating debris when a more precise contradiction—multi-trajectory motion, premature corrosion, reversed assembly, duplicated position, or broken sequence—can carry the card's identity.

### Known parallel expressions

- **迷离型 / Lost form:** a small causal void organizes a few incompatible biological, ceramic, and mechanical remnants. It crawls as if lost or pursues pockets of remaining order. Its crude shape is common local causal debris, not a juvenile or understandable animal.
- **飞翔型 / Multi-trajectory form:** it has no ordinary wings. One body occupies several incompatible aerial trajectories and then arrives at one position without traversing the intervening path. Fragments continuously rewrite their before-and-after relationships.
- **咆哮型 / Disorder-wave form:** its “roar” is a causal-disorder front that arrives before the sound. Fasteners detach, structures corrode, memory and action order fail, and only afterward does an echo occur without a matching vocal organ.
- **空亡时主 / Time Lord:** the singular causal core and stabilizing attractor of one major Time-Death catastrophe. It is neither an evolved form nor a conventional leader. Its void organizes the other parallel expressions, temporal fractures, and disorder phenomena; destroying it allows the event to lose coherence.

### Art ambition by rarity

Rarity controls compositional ambition and the visible radius of instability, not a biological growth ladder. Common art isolates one readable contradiction. Rare art shows a specific locomotion or environmental failure. Epic art layers a wider disorder process. Legendary art depicts a singular catastrophe-defining causal structure with clear scale and a story-specific composition.

### Human / Hollow-Null contradictions

A person such as Drake is not undergoing biological conversion by a species. Depict mutually incompatible states of that same person compressed around a causal void. Preserve the recognizable face, signature clothing, equipment, and deliberate behavior needed to show surviving identity, while other body states occupy misaligned positions, grow and erode simultaneously, or skip between ages. Avoid a clean vertical “human half / monster half” split and do not imply a stable hybrid lineage.

Use the environment's own palette and materials, with controlled rim light only to reveal edges; greater threat should appear as wider instability, not heavier armor or biological evolution. ## 噗噜兽 / Puru Beast

The current `FNG-017` artwork is the approved cub form, not the future charging adult form. A cub has a rounded seafoam-and-cream body, four short sturdy legs, glossy dark eyes with cyan star-shaped catchlights, cyan cheek freckles, small incisors, coral-lined sensory ear-fins, and a row of pearlescent dorsal magnetic nodules. Keep it clearly juvenile and curious. A future 冲撞噗噜兽 should retain these species markers while developing a heavier adult frame, stronger shoulders, a lower charging posture, and visibly impact-oriented behavior.
