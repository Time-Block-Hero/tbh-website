# Original species art direction

Use this reference when a card name, tribe, or artwork brief involves one of the project's original lifeforms. Preserve the shared visual grammar of its species while allowing individual silhouettes, occupations, environments, colors, and rarity-specific staging to vary.

## Puru Beasts (`奇兽`)

Puru Beasts are warm, placid extraterrestrial herd animals best understood as "space capybaras." Their canonical visual anchors are the approved young-adult artwork for `FNG-017 横行噗噜兽` at `../../../../assets/card-art/rampaging-puru-beast/rampaging-puru-beast-01.png` and the approved mature-adult artwork for `FNG-021 货运噗噜兽` at `../../../../assets/card-art/cargo-puru-beast/cargo-puru-beast-01.png`. Future Puru artwork should use the age-appropriate official image as a species-identity reference in addition to the project's default style template.

Lock the following recognition features across individuals: a compact barrel-shaped body with four short sturdy legs; a blunt capybara-like muzzle with small visible incisors; large glossy dark eyes with cyan star-shaped catchlights; two tall flexible sensory ear-fins with coral inner surfaces; four to six pearlescent magnetic nodules running along the upper back; and cyan bioluminescent cheek freckles that brighten with emotion. The baseline coat is pale seafoam or mint with a warm cream facial mask and underside, although markings and saturation may vary between individuals. Keep the silhouette soft, huggable, and slightly cartooned while retaining tactile fur, grounded weight, and believable alien anatomy.

Puru Beasts do not have an elastic juvenile phase and do not store impact. They are naturally sturdy, good-natured animals. Fast individuals can sprint or charge with surprising determination; cargo animals carry modular racks attached to their dorsal magnetic nodules.

Age is communicated through ordinary growth and proportion rather than metamorphosis. Younger Puru have rounder heads, larger eyes relative to the face, shorter muzzles, lighter bodies, and more exuberant expressions. Mature adults are substantially larger and heavier, with a long barrel torso, broad chest and shoulders, thicker legs and neck, a slightly longer and wider muzzle, smaller eye-to-head ratio, larger dorsal nodules, and calm experienced expressions. Adults remain appealing and gentle, but must not look like oversized babies. Cargo Puru should read immediately as mature working animals capable of carrying bulky modular freight without strain.

Never reduce a Puru to a slime, a literal Earth capybara, a generic mascot, or a rabbit-like creature. Do not replace the ear-fins with ordinary mammal ears, omit the dorsal nodules and star freckles, or introduce horns. For a family or herd scene, maintain the same facial grammar and alien markers while varying age, size, markings, and equipment.

## Crystal Spirits (`晶灵`)

A Crystal Spirit's true mind is self-organizing photon and electromagnetic resonance stabilized inside crystal lattices. Wisdom is granted through "crystal kindling" by higher Crystal Spirits whose origin is a living mother planet made of crystal. Ordinary star-energy ore is a possible vessel, not automatically a conscious being.

Every Crystal Spirit has a consciousness-bearing core plus separate crystals moving on atom-like inclined orbits, joined only by light and resonance. It has no required face, limbs, fixed mineral, fixed color, fixed core shape, or fixed shard count. Convey awareness through poised orbital motion, refraction, and responsive light rather than humanoid anatomy. Ordinary spirits are peaceful observers; high-order Oracle Spirits can interpret temporal resonance and answer questions like prophecy stones. They read possible futures rather than a single predetermined future.

## Nebula Manifestations (`星云体`)

Nebula Manifestations are executors of cosmic order: local embodiments through which the universe observes and enforces its own laws. They are not a political empire and need not share mortal morality. Each manifestation executes a principle such as gravity, stellar birth, distance, conservation, collapse, or correction of temporal anomalies.

They should be expensive, vast, and usually Epic or Legendary. A battlefield summon is only a locally sustained projection of the true cosmic-scale being. Give every manifestation a compact brilliant will-core, a translucent body made of nebulae, dust, and stars, gravitational arcs or spatial lensing, dissolving outer edges, and tiny ships, stations, or worlds as scale cues.

Their silhouettes are deliberately unconstrained: headless cosmic serpents, nebula manta rays, stellar wings or hands, ring beings grown around black holes, abstract giants made from star clusters, whale-like volumes without literal animal organs, and other original cosmic anatomies are all valid. Capture celestial grandeur without copying an existing cosmic dragon's head, horns, limbs, or branded design.

## Avatars (`兽裔(Avatar)`)

Avatars are human-descended people whose manifestation genes come from a single sacred God Tree. The Tree assigns every person an individual adaptive gene pattern; animal, draconic, or elf-like traits are not chosen as fashion and do not need to match the person's parents. The assigned manifestation is functional and permanent once expressed. Their human origin should remain readable through sapient personality, upright social body language, culture, clothing, tools, and technology; it does not require a human face.

Avatar manifestation has at least two intentional visual modes. A subtle or elf-like Avatar may retain a mostly human face and body with pointed ears, unusual pupils, horns, scales, or other restrained traits, but use this mode only when the card brief explicitly calls for it. A furry-form Avatar must be a complete anthropomorphic furry-subculture character, never merely a human with animal ears, a tail, or a few patches of fur.

For furry-form Avatars, establish a clearly nonhuman cranial silhouette with a species-appropriate muzzle or snout, nose, jaw, ears, horns, and expressive anime/furry eyes. Carry the manifestation coherently across the whole body with full fur, scales, feathers, or patterned hide; paws or claws, palm and foot pads where appropriate; digitigrade or otherwise species-specific legs and feet; and a clearly readable tail or equivalent silhouette feature. Preserve humanoid hands, posture, facial acting, fashion sense, and equipment use so the character reads as a person rather than a literal Earth animal or mascot.

Furry-form presentation may range from soft and elegant to colorful streetwear, athletic and dynamic, or heroic and muscular. Intentional coat colors and markings, styled head fur or hair, jewelry, and bold clothing are part of the subcultural visual language. Sensual styling is a character and costume choice, not a racial requirement; match pose, coverage, age, and tone to the individual card. Tailor industrial sci-fi clothing and gear to the anatomy with tail openings, digitigrade footwear, ear and horn clearance, and claw-friendly gloves rather than fitting an ordinary human costume over the body.

Use one coherent assigned animal, draconic, or elf-like archetype as the dominant manifestation. Mixed-species forms are valid only when the card brief or God Tree assignment explicitly calls for a hybrid; do not assemble random attractive traits. Make adaptations useful in context, such as directional ears, low-light eyes, balancing tails, thermal fur, pressure-sensing horns, climbing feet, or radiation-resistant scales.

Avoid human faces with costume ears, localized fur pasted onto otherwise human anatomy, generic orcs, random hybridization, photoreal taxidermy, and mascot or chibi caricature unless the card explicitly requests it. Reference images may establish anatomy, color confidence, fashion energy, and furry-subculture appeal, but never copy a specific character, watermark, outfit, or pose.

The God Tree's proper name, how it communicates across interstellar distance, and how off-world births receive an assignment remain open lore decisions. Do not invent definitive answers without user confirmation.

## Hollow-Nulls (`空亡体`)

Hollow-Nulls are causal debris produced when mutually contradictory timeline states are compressed together by a time rift. They embody chaos and rising entropy. They consume order rather than flesh: matter rusts, structures forget their connections, organisms age or regress, and actions lose causal continuity around them.

Every Hollow-Null has a central causal void that reflects no light, anatomy or material fragments that do not align, limbs occupying contradictory temporal positions, simultaneous growth and erosion, broken nearby lines and lighting, and discontinuous frame-skipping movement. Avoid default black-purple tentacle monsters; make the horror come from structures that cannot logically coexist.

Increasing power means increasing destructive instability, not greater bodily toughness. Low forms are confused causal scraps. Flying forms occupy several incompatible trajectories instead of using ordinary wings. Roaring forms convert nearby structure into disorder before their sound arrives.

Every `空亡时主` is the core of a specific Time-Death catastrophe. It is the disaster's stabilizing attractor rather than merely its largest monster: lesser Hollow-Nulls and local time failures organize around it. Destroying the Time Lord collapses the causal void, disperses the associated creatures, and allows local time to resume, thereby ending that catastrophe. Treat it as a Legendary clarity-gate subject. Its art must show the unique disaster it anchors, a dominant causal-void core, the surrounding world failing in contradictory time states, and the decisive possibility that killing this singular entity can still save the region.
